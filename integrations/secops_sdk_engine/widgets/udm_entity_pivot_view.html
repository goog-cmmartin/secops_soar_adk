How to Use UDM & Raw Log Entity Pivot

This widget extracts case/alert entities, automatically infers Chronicle UDM field groups, and pivots directly into Chronicle SIEM Search.

  • Entity Type & Tag Filter Bars: Click any entity type chip or tag chip (Suspicious, Pivot, Internal, Manual, Untagged) to show or hide matching entities. Click Tagged Only to hide untagged entities in one click.
  • Sortable Columns: Click the Identifier, Type, or Tags column headers to sort ascending (▲), descending (▼), or reset to case order (⇅).
  • UDM & RLS Buttons: Click UDM for a structured field-group search (expanding emails to short usernames and FQDNs to short hostnames) or RLS for a regex raw log scan.
  • Copy Query (📋): Click the copy icon next to any entity or in the toolbar to copy the generated UDM query to your clipboard.
  • Compound Search: Select 2 or more entities, choose AND or OR, select a time window (±6h to ±7d), and click Run Compound UDM.
